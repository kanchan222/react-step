How to use Canva’s AI Image Generator

  1. Add your prompt

    In the AI image generator's prompt box, describe a detailed prompt name the subject, the setting, and the mood. The more specific your text, the more captivating the result.

  2. Pick an art style and aspect ratio

    Choose from different art styles like Vibrant, Sketch, Pop Art, Photo, and more. Set your aspect ratio to your desired image size.

  3. Generate your AI image

    Click the "Generate AI image" button to start using the AI image generator. Log in or sign up when prompted. Your image will appear in seconds.

  4. Preview and refine the image

    Browse through the generated image variations. Then, tweak your prompt and tap “Regenerate” until one nails your desired look.

  5. Download or keep designing in Canva

    Save your image as a PNG, JPG, or PDF file on your device. Or click "Open in editor" to add text, layers, and effects for a finished design in the Canva editor.
